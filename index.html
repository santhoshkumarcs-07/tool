🔒 Privacy & Architecture Notice

          

            How your video data and transcripts are securely processed.
          

        

        
      

      

        

          100% Server-Side Local Processing: Audio extraction (FFmpeg), speech-to-text (Faster-Whisper), and frame OCR (Tesseract) run strictly on the server backend, not on your personal device or mobile phone.
        

        
What data is sent to OpenRouter?

        

          Only the generated text transcript is sent to the configured OpenRouter AI gateway for semantic discovery of developer tools, MCP servers, and technical skills. Raw video and audio files are never transmitted to third-party AI models.
        

        
Zero Paid Model Guarantee

        

          The backend connects exclusively through OpenRouter's free model pool (openrouter/free, Llama 3.3 70B, Qwen 2.5 Coder, Gemini 2.0 Flash) with automatic zero-cost fallback. Zero credit card or paid API calls are ever made.
        

        
Data Storage & Cleanup

        

          Intermediate audio extracts (WAV) are deleted immediately upon transcription. Stored video uploads and transcripts are isolated per-session and can be deleted from your saved library at any time.
